🎱 8-Ball Pool

Power 55%

How to play

Drag on the table to aim; the dotted line shows where the white ball goes and which way the ball you hit will roll (red means it is not your ball). Set the power and tap Shoot. Use ◀ ▶ for tiny aim changes.

After the break, the first player to pot a ball gets that group: solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15). Pot all of yours, then the black 8-ball to win. Potting the 8 too early, or with a foul, loses the game.

Fouls give the other player the white ball “in hand”: drag it anywhere, then shoot. Fouls are: potting the white ball, hitting the wrong ball first, hitting nothing, or no ball reaching a cushion after the hit.
