Overview

Drop Social — usually called Drops — is a social platform built around posting, real-time group chat and live audio, with a rewards layer that pays participants in its own token. The pitch is ownership: creators keep more of what they create, and communities run their own spaces instead of renting attention from an algorithm.

Functionally it combines several products people already use separately: a feed (posts, photos, short video, polls, threads), creator-run group chats called Channels, live audio rooms called Drop Calls, direct messages, tipping, creator stores, and promotional campaigns. The token, DROPS, is a BEP-20 asset on BNB Smart Chain with a fixed maximum supply of 100,000,000.

This page is an independent educational overview written by XpertCreation. It is not an official Drops page, not a listing, and not investment advice.

How it works

Feed

Text, up to 4 photos, video up to a minute, polls and threads, with a “see new drops” pill so the post you are reading does not jump.

Channels

Real-time group chats around a creator or topic. Public or invite-only, with owners, mods, slow mode and chat lock.

Drop Calls

Live audio rooms: one host, two co-hosts, up to twelve speakers, unlimited listeners. Raise a hand to take the mic.

Economy

Tips between users, activity-based rewards, campaign tasks and referral bonuses, plus optional premium badges.

How rewards are earned and claimed

  1. Earn in-app — posting, engaging, completing campaign tasks, receiving tips, referrals.
  2. Complete verification and 2FA — required before any withdrawal becomes possible.
  3. Add your own BSC wallet — a wallet you control, on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20).
  4. Claim above the minimum threshold — test with a small amount first.
  5. Move or convert as you choose — send to an exchange on the correct network, or swap on a BNB Chain DEX.
Network discipline: sending a BEP-20 token to an address expecting a different network usually destroys it. Verify the network on both sides, every time, and keep a little BNB for gas.

What to watch, honestly

  • Liquidity and price risk. New tokens trade thinly; a quoted price is not a price you can necessarily exit at.
  • Reward dilution. Activity-based reward rates in any platform can change as participation grows.
  • Verification cost. Some platform features require a paid verification step — budget for it or skip those features entirely.
  • Referral incentives. Invite links reward the inviter. That is normal, but it means you should judge the product on its own, not on who sent you the link.
  • Impersonation. Lookalike apps and fake support accounts are the most common loss vector in this sector. Install only from the official store listing.
