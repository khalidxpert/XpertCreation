🏏 Cricket

Chase the target! Each level gives you a number of balls and wickets. Time your shot when the ball reaches you.


How to play

Watch the bowler run in and tap a shot when the ball reaches your bat. Timing is everything: perfect timing sends it to the boundary; early shots go to the leg side (left), late shots to the off side (right); very early or late and you miss.

Defend keeps you safe. Drive hits along the ground for 1 to 4. Loft goes in the air for 6, but can be caught. Tapping the pitch also plays a Drive. On a keyboard: ↑ loft, → drive, ↓ defend.

If you miss a ball heading for the stumps, you are bowled. Win a level to open the next one; the targets get bigger and the bowling gets trickier (yorkers, bouncers, slower balls and spin).
