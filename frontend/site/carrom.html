⚪ Carrom

How to play

Slide the striker along your baseline, drag on the board to aim (you can only shoot forward), set the power and tap Shoot. A red ring means the striker is touching a coin: move it.

You play white, the computer black. Pocket your coin and you play again. The red queen can be pocketed once you have pocketed a coin of your own, and you must “cover” it by pocketing one of your coins on the next shot, or it goes back to the centre.

Pocketing the striker is a foul: one of your pocketed coins returns to the centre. The queen must be pocketed before anyone’s last coin. Clear all your coins to win the board: you score one point for each coin your opponent still has, plus 3 if you covered the queen.
