What the platform is

Drops is a social platform operated by Yalafi Technology. It combines a feed, creator-run group chats (Channels), live audio rooms (Drop Calls), direct messages, tipping, and creator stores. It is free to use on Android, iOS and the web, with optional premium badges.

Binance Pay: how the money actually moves

The important thing to understand first: there is no "withdraw to Binance Pay" button inside Drops. Rewards are claimed as DROPS — a BEP-20 token on BNB Smart Chain — to a wallet you control. Binance Pay is a separate transfer that happens after your funds reach Binance.

  1. Verify and enable 2FA inside Drops. The claim stays locked without it, whatever the balance shows.
  2. Add your own BSC wallet address. Confirm the network reads BNB Smart Chain / BEP-20 before saving it.
  3. Claim a small test amount first, confirm it arrives, then claim the rest.
  4. Convert if needed. If your exchange does not list DROPS, swap it on a BNB Chain DEX to USDT or BNB.
  5. Deposit to Binance on BEP-20. In Binance choose Deposit, pick the token, select BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20), copy that address only.
  6. Use Binance Pay for the last step. Once the balance is on Binance, send it to any Binance user by Pay ID or QR — instant, no network fee.
The one mistake that costs everything: sending a BEP-20 token to an address expecting a different network. Those funds are usually gone for good. Read the network name on both sides before every transfer, and always test with a small amount first. Keep a little BNB for gas, or you will hold tokens you cannot move.
