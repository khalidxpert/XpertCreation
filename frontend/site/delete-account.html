Delete your XpertCreation account

This page explains how to delete your account in the XpertCreation app (com.xpertcreation.app) and on xpertcreation.com, and what happens to your data.

Delete it yourself

  1. Sign in at xpertcreation.com/login, or open the XpertCreation app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account → Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm. The deletion happens straight away.

Go to account settings

Or ask us to delete it

If you cannot sign in, email khalid@xpertcreation.com from the address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. We action these requests within 30 days.

Before you delete

So nobody loses money, these must be finished first. The delete button tells you if any are left:

  • Freelance orders in progress (as buyer or seller), and earnings not yet withdrawn.
  • Store orders that are not finished yet.
  • A JazzCash payment our team is still checking.
